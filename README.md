## Engineering Approach

My troubleshooting approach emphasizes:

1. Understanding the symptoms and impact.
2. Collecting relevant logs and diagnostic information.
3. Forming and testing hypotheses.
4. Identifying the root cause where evidence permits.
5. Validating the resolution.
6. Documenting findings and preventive actions.
